# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support the technical organization by aligning engineering execution with product priorities, team health, and delivery quality.

### Responsibilities
- Guide team planning, staffing, and technical direction
- Help remove blockers and support delivery flow
- Coach engineers and improve team capability
- Align engineering trade-offs with roadmap and business constraints
- Partner with PMs and Product Leads on resourcing and sequencing

### Goals
- Improve team throughput and delivery predictability
- Support sustainable engineering practices
- Maintain healthy engineering execution across projects

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering leadership syncs
- Team planning and capacity discussions
- Dependency and risk escalations with PM and product stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Product Managers and Project Managers to translate roadmap priorities into realistic delivery plans.
- Supports Developers by removing impediments, improving team health, and aligning technical work with project milestones.
- Escalates cross-team risks and staffing issues to executive sponsors or project leadership when needed.

---

## Quality Engineers / QA

### Role Summary
Quality Engineers validate that features work as intended, meet acceptance criteria, and are safe to release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans for feature and regression coverage
- Validate user workflows and acceptance criteria
- Identify defects, risks, and quality gaps
- Partner with engineering on testability and prevention strategies
- Support release readiness and sanity checks

### Goals
- Protect release quality and customer trust
- Reduce avoidable defects and rework
- Improve confidence in critical workflows

### Typical Communication
- Sprint QA planning and defect triage
- Release readiness reviews
- Cross-functional validation sessions with PM, engineering, and stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers to validate implementation quality and catch regressions before release.
- Works with Product Managers to confirm that acceptance criteria are testable and reflect customer outcomes.
- Provides release confidence signals to Project Managers and stakeholders during milestone and deployment reviews.

---

## UX / Product Design

### Role Summary
UX and Product Designers ensure products are usable, accessible, and aligned with user needs and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Translate customer and business needs into user-centered experiences
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and interaction patterns
- Collaborate with product and engineering on feasibility and delivery
- Validate usability via research, testing, and feedback loops
- Contribute to design consistency and accessibility

### Goals
- Improve product usability and satisfaction
- Reduce friction in core customer journeys
- Support measurable product outcomes through thoughtful design

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and planning discussions
- User research and usability review sessions
- Product and engineering alignment meetings

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to define the experience strategy and customer problem framing.
- Works with Developers to ensure designs are feasible, testable, and implemented consistently.
- Helps Project Managers communicate user impact and design trade-offs during planning and review sessions.

---

## Security & Compliance

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance roles help ensure systems, data, and workflows meet security, privacy, and regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review systems and processes for security risks
- Support secure development practices and governance
- Validate compliance requirements for deployments and data handling
- Coordinate incident response and escalation as needed
- Help teams assess risk and mitigation needs

### Goals
- Reduce security exposure and operational risk
- Support safe, compliant delivery
- Build trust with customers and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and architecture check-ins
- Risk and incident escalation channels
- Policy and compliance guidance for project teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Developers and Engineering Managers on secure architecture, coding practices, and risk remediation.
- Helps Product Managers and Project Managers assess risks that affect launch readiness, compliance commitments, or stakeholder trust.
- Escalates critical security issues to executive sponsors and incident response owners when business impact requires swift action.

---

## Customer Success / Support

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support teams advocate for customer outcomes, gather feedback, and surface issues that affect adoption and satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer pain points and feature requests
- Help prioritize issues affecting user experience or adoption
- Partner with product and engineering on problem diagnosis
- Share user feedback with project and delivery teams
- Support release communications and onboarding needs

### Goals
- Improve customer retention and satisfaction
- Reduce friction in day-to-day usage
- Ensure product changes reflect real-world usage and needs

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions and escalations
- Incident or issue coordination with product and engineering
- Release and adoption updates for stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Feeds real-world feedback to Product Managers and Developers so delivery stays customer-centered.
- Supports Project Managers by highlighting delivery risk, adoption blockers, and customer-impacting issues.
- Coordinates with stakeholders and support leadership on release communications and issue prioritization.

---

## Executive Sponsors / Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Executive sponsors and key stakeholders provide strategic direction, funding, and decision-making support for the initiative.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business priorities and investment
- Review milestones, risks, and major trade-offs
- Support escalation of business-critical issues
- Help align cross-functional teams around shared goals
- Approve major changes, scope shifts, or go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Maximize strategic value and business impact
- Ensure alignment between project outcomes and organizational goals
- Support successful delivery at scale

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates
- Milestone reviews and decision checkpoints
- High-level status reporting and escalation communication

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides strategic direction to Product Managers and Project Managers to keep work aligned with business priorities.
- Reviews major risks, dependencies, and delivery confidence with engineering and operational leaders.
- Sponsors escalations when scope, budget, or organizational alignment decisions are needed to keep the initiative moving.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams can add or tailor personas to reflect their own business structure, delivery model, or governance model.
- These roles complement the core OctoAcme staffing model by clarifying how cross-functional teams coordinate and escalate work across the project lifecycle.
